This example estimates routine capacity needed as the weekly routine forecast × the selected wait in weeks, less routine bookings already made in that window. It does not model an existing waiting list, booking patterns, clinician eligibility, working patterns or daily variations in available slots. Weekly capacity is pooled: daily figures do not establish whether the right clinicians or slots are available on each day. A weekly forecast alone cannot determine future patient waiting times. Same-day “embargoed” slots are reserved, not open for booking.

In a real version, recommendations would need to use the imported report and live forecasts, identify gaps or stale data, and be reviewed against the clinical system before any slot changes.
